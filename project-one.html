01Context

Campaign: Reading the Letters

Objective:

Grow Vertus Group's audience among the Constellation Software network and founder-operators looking to learn from proven business thinking.

Marketing brainstorm:

Mark Leonard's President's Letters, written by Constellation's founder and former CEO, carry real historical weight and reflect the company's long-term philosophy. I used them as the hook to draw in new readers and raise Vertus Group's visibility.

02My role

What I did

  • Built user personas mapping each audience's goals, motivations and pain points
  • Shaped the content around those motivations, closing each piece with a light call to action
  • Wrote 3 blog articles and 3 companion social posts
  • Owned the campaign end to end, from brainstorm and execution through performance analysis

04Takeaways

What I learned

Know your audience deeply

Personas set the tone, structure and content. Once the intent is clear, the right material follows.

Handle sensitive information carefully

Working with company history meant balancing storytelling with confidentiality.

Distribution matters as much as creation

Adapting content for each platform extended the campaign's reach.
