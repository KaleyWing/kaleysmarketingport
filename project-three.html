Bloom Designathon

Campaign Objective

Promoting the event of a one day designathon host by the two biggest design student clubs in UWaterloo, by creating attention-grabbing event content that reaches the right audience, sharing key details, and driving sign-ups and attendance.

Marketing brainstorm

Our thought process: Creating teasers to hint at collaboration + Introducing our event with a major trend (something that never fails) + two reels on the day of (interviewing attendees) + recap post
